Track to Graph

A local, browser-only 2D motion analysis tool.

Choose a video to begin.

2. Scale

Click the two ends of a known distance in the video, then enter that distance.

Load a video first.

3. Track

Draw a box around one distinctive object on the current video frame.

Set the scale to unlock tracking.

4. Analyze

Finish tracking to see graphs.

Analysis assumptions

The camera is fixed, movement is approximately parallel to the image plane, perspective distortion is negligible, and downscreen is gravity. The first tracked point is the physical origin. No regression or smoothing is applied.
